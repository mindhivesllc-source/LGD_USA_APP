# Content Guidelines

**Source:** https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/design/content

## Required Polaris Components & Patterns
- Follow Polaris **voice and tone** guidelines
- Follow Polaris **grammar and mechanics** guidelines

## Voice and Tone
- Voice: consistent across all contexts and audiences
- Tone: changes based on audience and context
- Reference: Polaris voice and tone guidelines

## Product Content Principles

### Use Plain Language
- Target **US grade 7 reading level** (use Hemingway App to check)
- Use headings, bullets, and short sentences for scannability
- Avoid jargon or overly technical language
- Avoid large blocks of text
- Avoid idioms and phrases with indirect/ironic meanings (mistranslation risk)

### Be Consistent
- Use **single noun/verb/phrase** for each concept/action
- **Eliminate synonyms** for key concepts and actions
- Avoid multiple synonyms for same thing (e.g., don't use both "Upload images" and "Add photos")

### Encourage Action
- CTAs: start with **strong verb** describing the action
- Use **active voice** to clarify subject and action
- Prioritize most important information and tasks
- Break complicated tasks into steps focused on individual actions

### Guide, Don't Prescribe
- Put merchant at center and in control
- Give enough information for merchants to make their own decisions

### Avoid Duplication
- Don't duplicate page title under Title bar
- Don't add horizontal navigation when using App nav
- Don't present same content in different ways on same page

## Grammar and Mechanics
- Use proper grammar throughout
- First reference: use proper app/company name
- Subsequent references in same section: use "we"
- Follow Polaris grammar and mechanics for spelling, punctuation, dates, numbers, measurements

## Hard Requirements for Design Compliance
- Content at grade 7 reading level
- Single term per concept (no synonyms)
- No duplicated content on same page
- No jargon, idioms, or large text blocks
- CTAs must lead with strong verbs in active voice
