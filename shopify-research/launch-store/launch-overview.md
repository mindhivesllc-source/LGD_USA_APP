# Launch Overview

Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch

## The 5-Phase Launch Journey

Shopify structures the launch process into 5 phases:

### 1. Checklist Review
- Check for quality and understand requirements
- Must meet [App Store requirements](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/shopify-app-store/app-store-requirements)

### 2. Pricing Strategy
- Determine a strategy for how you charge merchants
- Must use Shopify App Pricing or the Shopify Billing API

### 3. Deployment
- Deploy to your own hosting provider as well as Shopify
- Can deploy to Google Cloud Run, any hosting service, or use Shopify's versioned deployments

### 4. Distribution and Review
- Specify how you'll distribute your app
- Submit your app for review
- App goes through Draft → Submitted → Reviewed → Published statuses

### 5. Merchant Support
- App developers must be responsible stewards of their apps
- Provide support, emergency contact, and handle sunsets properly

## Key Benefits of the Shopify App Store
- **0% revenue share on first $1,000,000 USD** in annual gross app revenue
- Built-in audience of millions of merchants
- **Built for Shopify certification** gives significant competitive edge

## Key Sub-Sections
- **Quality Assurance**: Going live, App Store requirements, Built for Shopify, Privacy, Protected customer data
- **Pricing Strategy**: About billing, Shopify App Pricing, Manual pricing (legacy), Billing adjustments
- **Deployment**: About deployment, Google Cloud Run, Hosting services, App versions, CD pipeline
- **Reaching Customers**: Distribution, Support, Sunsetting, Go-to-market, Usage tracking, Visibility, Revenue share
- **App Store Review**: Review process, Submission, Pass review, Categories, Policy violations, Quality checks
- **Shopify App Store Ads**: About ads, Create/manage ads, Performance, Billing, Permissions
- **Marketing**: About marketing, Press releases, Brand assets, Listing traffic, Manage app reviews
